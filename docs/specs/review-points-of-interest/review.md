Review of [PR #1217](https://github.com/Kapps/weavie/pull/1217) at `6c647398` against [design.md](design.md): a
`weavie-reviewer` pass, then a duplication pass over the whole diff. Every finding was checked against the code.
The ones marked *observed* were also reproduced in the running app, by running a suite, or in the PR's CI run.
Nothing was fixed in the review stage; the fix stage's outcome is in each Status.

**Recheck at `4d9f4874`.** All six bug fixes held and CI went green. W6 was reopened, and the fixes added W7, N11
and N12.

hold. The W7 fix holds for Escape but broke stepping after a dismiss (B7). The user then reviewed the header in the
app and reported four problems, recorded as B8, W8, W9 and W10 with measurements. Nothing was fixed in the recheck.

W8 and W10 hold, and B8's boxes are centred as claimed. The rebuild broke three things, recorded as W11 to W13, and
reopened W3 in a new form. Nothing was fixed in the recheck.

**Recheck at `e1689e89`.** The diff was read and the card, popover and header measured in the running app. W3,
W11, W12, W13 and N14 hold. The W3 fix moved the stepper's count 1px below the text beside it in the hint popover
(W14). Nothing was fixed in the recheck.

**Recheck at `aa3912bf`.** The diff was read and the header, card and popover measured again. W14's target holds
(the popover's count is level with the text beside it) and the two test fixes hold. The same change moved the
stepper's arrows 1px low in all three places and made the header 1px taller (W16). Nothing was fixed in the recheck.
